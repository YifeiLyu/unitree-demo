Controls

  • Stand behind the robot, facing the same way. Your forward is captured on entering.
  • Hold a grip to move that arm. The robot hand follows your hand relative to where you grabbed. Twist the controller to roll the wrist.
  • Let go and the arm stays put. Grab again somewhere comfortable to keep going.
  • A or X = switch arms: both → left only → right only. The arm that is not selected stays where it is.
  • B or Y (tap) = FREEZE: both arms hold immediately. Release both grips, then grab again to resume.
  • Hold B or Y for 1 second = E-STOP, same as the dashboard: the robot lets go of the arms (be ready to support them). Re-engage on the dashboard afterwards.
  • Click a thumbstick = re-centre forward to where you are looking.
  • Point at the status panel or the green robot model and hold the trigger to drag it. While dragging, push the thumbstick forward/back to move it farther/closer.
  • Poses that would hit the body, the head, the other hand or the base are refused (the controller buzzes). The dashboard E-STOP still works.
