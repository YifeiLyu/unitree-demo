Controls

  • Stand behind the robot, facing the same way. Your forward is captured on entering.
  • Hold a grip to move that arm. The robot hand follows your hand relative to where you grabbed. Twist the controller to roll the wrist.
  • Let go and the arm stays put. Grab again somewhere comfortable to keep going.
  • B or Y = FREEZE: both arms hold immediately. Release both grips, then grab again to resume.
  • Click a thumbstick = re-centre forward to where you are looking.
  • Poses that would hit the body, the head, the other hand or the base are refused (the controller buzzes). The dashboard E-STOP still works.
